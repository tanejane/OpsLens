# Business analyst assessment

|Question|What the current implementation can support|Evidence needed before trusting the answer|
|---|---|---|
|Why did revenue decrease last week?|Finance compilers support explicit bounded dates. The dashboard defaults to a historical calendar month. Relative weeks need a reporting date, timezone, week convention and data coverage.|Return exact current and comparison intervals, source coverage and amounts. Clarify the anchor when the historical data ends before the requested week.|
|Why did profit fall while revenue rose?|dashboard_api.assemble_dashboard flags the direction. agent/phase4c4_drivers.py reconciles effective revenue, COGS, named cost movements and an adjustment residual. The engine can reject an unconfirmed premise.|Reconcile both periods to independent totals and show each signed contribution and residual. An accounting contribution is measurable even when the commercial reason for it remains unknown.|
|Which products or customers drove the drop?|agent/phase4c19_product.py measures an approved category at item grain, including units, item GMV and share. Seller delivery and refund concentration have narrow follow-ups. These are different measures from recognized revenue or profit.|A general contribution ranking requires the same measure and eligible population across periods, a complete partition including unknowns, and a sum of segment deltas that matches the overall delta. No general customer-segment bridge was established.|
|How did average order value change?|The governed finance registry exposes placed orders and effective revenue from different sources. The product registry exposes average item price. Neither supplies a general, validated AOV comparison.|Define the numerator and matching eligible orders, cancellation/refund policy and purchase-time basis. Test zero orders, missing inputs and partial coverage.|
|Which metrics explain an anomaly?|Cross-domain routing aligns selected purchase months and can show operational metrics alongside finance. Exact order-ID reconciliation applies only to delivery/review base populations for the saved intervals.|Show counts for eligible reviews and deliveries separately. Evidence of coincident movements cannot establish causation or equal finance populations.|
|Can I trust the report?|Canonical results preserve periods, units, validated evidence and limitations. The replay path returns a stored snapshot. Ambiguous sales can trigger clarification. Missing dashboard readings remain unavailable.|Each decision-relevant number needs source identity, population, period and reproducible reconciliation. Replay verifies saved content, not the freshness or correctness of upstream data.|

Finance margins use ratios of sums with a guarded denominator. Calendar comparisons use half-open boundaries. Review creation date in the earlier finance compiler and purchase-month review metrics in the satisfaction compiler answer different questions. Display the time basis with both readings. Item GMV excludes freight. Customer review scores, item averages, placed orders and financially represented orders should never share a denominator by implication.

The strongest use today is a scoped historical comparison with a visible evidence trail. The saved acceptance run cannot support a promise of general root-cause discovery. For this product, executable period semantics, independent totals and a reconciling segment bridge have higher value than adding anomaly models.
